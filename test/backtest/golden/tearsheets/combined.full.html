Method, assumptions & warnings

How the figures were computed

Returns are time weighted: cash flows move the equity but are not returns. Each IST session date contributes one return, from the last equity point of the date.

Volatility, Sharpe, Sortino and alpha are annualised with 248.17 sessions a year: the NSE calendar in 2025 to 2026, holiday data sourced. CAGR uses elapsed calendar time.

Risk-free rate 0.00% a year, compounded down to 0.0000% a session.

Every drawdown figure is a fall, so it is zero or negative. The benchmark's figures are measured as the strategy's are, on its known session returns. Each metric's definition shows on its label; every definition cites its source in the documentation of openalgo.backtest.metrics.
